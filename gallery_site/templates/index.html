Ski Area Historical Archive

A developing archive documenting the evolution of ski areas across North America through trail maps, aerial imagery, lift systems, ownership history, archival collections, oral histories, and lost-area evidence.

Resorts Collections Museums Trail Maps Lost Areas Timelines

Institutions & Collections

The archive now tracks museums, university archives, historical societies, digital repositories, and private collections as first-class sources.

Historical Snapshots

Each resort can be documented by year-specific snapshots combining maps, aerials, photos, lifts, ownership, and statistics.

Lost-Area Evidence

Closed and abandoned ski areas are tracked through lifecycle events, field evidence, historical maps, and local archival material.

GIS-Ready Future

Trail maps and aerial imagery are being structured for future boundary, lift-alignment, and trail-geometry comparisons.
